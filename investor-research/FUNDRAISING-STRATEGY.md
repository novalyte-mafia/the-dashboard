# Fundraising Strategy — Novalyte AI

**Prepared:** July 21, 2026. Based on actual verified stage: post-product, pre-revenue, solo founder, no prior outside capital.
**Not legal advice.** All instruments and terms below must be reviewed by a qualified startup attorney before signing anything.

---

## Stage-honest assessment

| Factor | Reality | Fundraising implication |
|---|---|---|
| Product | 7 modules live in production | Unusually strong for pre-seed; demo-able, verifiable |
| Revenue | $0 | Priced equity round unrealistic; SAFEs appropriate |
| Team | Solo founder | Biggest investor objection; address in use of funds |
| GTM | Founder-led clinic outreach in progress | Early numbers will move weekly — use in follow-ups |
| Market | Hot category (men's health, GLP-1) with visible public comps | "Why now" is credible |

**Conclusion: raise a pre-seed on SAFEs, anchored by operator-angels and micro-VCs, in parallel with a 30-day traction sprint.** Do not attempt a priced seed with institutional lead requirements at $0 revenue.

## Recommended structure

- **Instrument:** YC post-money SAFE (standard, attorney-reviewed). Single cap for all investors in the round; avoid uncapped or MFN-only unless advised.
- **Target raise:** **$600k** (announce range "$500–750k")
- **Minimum viable round:** **$250k** — funds 12 months of the commercial motion without the engineering hire; decide hire vs. contractor at that level
- **Valuation cap:** set with attorney/advisor input after first 3–5 investor conversations; test the market before anchoring. Flag: cap, discount, pro-rata side letters, and any board/information rights all need attorney review.
- **Check sizes:** angels $10–50k · operator-angels/strategics $25–100k · micro-VCs/pre-seed funds $100–300k
- **Investor count:** roughly 1–2 micro-VC anchors ($150–300k) + 6–12 angels/operators ($10–50k) ≈ 8–14 checks
- **Runway target:** 18 months at ~$33k/month average burn

## Path options analyzed

| Path | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Angel + operator syndicate first** | **DO — primary** | Fastest yes at this stage; operator-angels add clinic intros and credibility that attract micro-VCs |
| **Micro-VC / pre-seed funds** | **DO — parallel** | Several explicitly back post-product/pre-revenue; anchor checks legitimize the round |
| **SAFE financing** | **DO — instrument** | Standard for this stage; cheap, fast, no board mechanics |
| **Accelerator (YC etc.)** | **APPLY opportunistically** | YC application costs nothing and the deadline discipline helps; do NOT block outreach waiting for it. If accepted, it likely replaces most of this round on better terms |
| **Strategic healthcare investor** | **LATER in round** | Clinic-network operators/MSO owners as strategic angel checks — yes; corporate VCs — too slow at pre-seed |
| **Friends & family** | **Optional first close** | Only if genuinely available and they understand the risk; helps momentum ("round is open and funded to $X") |
| **Revenue-funded clinic pilots** | **DO regardless** | 2–3 paid pilots ($500–2k/mo each) are worth more than any deck line; pursue during the raise |
| **Priced seed round** | **NOT NOW** | No revenue, solo founder — terms would be poor; earn the seed with this round's milestones |

## Use of funds ($600k / 18 months)

| Category | % | ~$ | What it buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial motion | 45% | $270k | Founder salary (modest), part-time clinic-success contractor, paid demand tests in 3–5 metros, tooling |
| First engineering hire | 35% | $210k | Senior full-stack (or 2 contractors) from ~month 3, so product velocity survives founder selling time |
| Legal, compliance, infra, buffer | 20% | $120k | Healthcare counsel (marketing compliance, anti-kickback review of lead routing), hosting, insurance, contingency |

## Milestones this round must hit (the seed story)

1. 100+ verified clinics live across 3–5 metros
2. 10–20 clinics actively using booking/lead flows
3. First 3–5 paying clinics; ≥$5k MRR-equivalent
4. 500+ completed patient assessments routed to clinics
5. Documented clinic CAC + activation playbook
6. 1 engineering hire made; founder ≥50% time on GTM

## Proof points needed BEFORE first pitches (this week)

1. Portal P1 fixes (see INVESTOR-PORTAL-AUDIT.md): footer legal rewording, founder bio + photo, fundraise page phrasing
2. Seed honest "Actual" metrics into the portal traction page (clinics in pipeline, profiles live, pages deployed)
3. One-page PDF overview uploaded to the data room

5. Decide the exact raise number and stick to it in every conversation

## Sequencing (recommended)

- **Week 1:** fixes above + first 5 highest-priority sends (see EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md) + YC application drafted
- **Weeks 2–3:** first outreach wave (top 10), 8–10 sends/day max across the rest of Tier 1/2; run traction sprint in parallel (30-DAY-TRACTION-PLAN.md)
- **Week 4+:** second wave with traction-update follow-ups; convert warm intros from Tier 3 operators
- **First close** when ~$150–250k is committed — momentum compounds; don't wait for the full amount to sign the first SAFEs (attorney handles the paperwork sequencing)

## Attorney-review flags (do not sign without counsel)

- SAFE cap/discount/MFN configuration and side letters
- Any investor requesting board seats, vetoes, or information rights at pre-seed (unusual; push back)
- Securities-law exemptions and accredited-investor verification for angels
- Healthcare-specific counsel on lead-routing economics (anti-kickback/fee-splitting adjacency) before monetizing clinic leads
