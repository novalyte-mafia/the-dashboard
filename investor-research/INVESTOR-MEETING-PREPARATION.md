# Investor Meeting Preparation — Novalyte AI

## The 20-minute meeting structure

| Minutes | Segment | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Personal + why them | One sentence on why you wanted *this* investor specifically (their portfolio/thesis) |
| 2–6 | Problem + wedge | Clinic-side fragmentation story; the free-listing wedge; the anti-Hims positioning |
| 6–12 | **Live product demo** | This is the differentiator — most pre-seed founders show decks; you show production |
| 12–16 | GTM + numbers | Command Center pipeline walk-through, current honest metrics, what calls are teaching you |
| 16–19 | Raise + use of funds | $500–750k SAFE, three-line budget, 18-month milestones |
| 19–20 | Ask | "Is this something you'd want to be part of? What would you need to see?" |

## Demo script (6 minutes, rehearse until smooth)

1. **novalyte.io/directory** (60s) — search a launch metro, open a verified clinic profile. Point: "every profile is permission-based and verified — this is the supply wedge."
2. **ads.novalyte.io campaign page** (90s) — scroll a landing page, start the embedded assessment, answer 2 questions. Point: "the assessment inherits page context — treatment, city, campaign — and routes the lead with full attribution."
3. **Workforce** (45s) — job board + a professional profile. Point: "clinics' second-biggest complaint after patient flow is hiring."
4. **Marketplace** (30s) — vendor categories. Point: "third revenue layer, same clinic relationship."
5. **admin.novalyte.io Command Center** (90s) — prospect list, call console, outreach history. Point: "this is how I run GTM personally — every call is logged and feeds product."
6. **investor.novalyte.io** (30s) — "everything we discussed is documented here, including what's *not* done — statuses are honest."

**Demo hygiene:** test all five URLs the morning of the meeting; have screenshots as backup for screen-share failures; never demo the founder-admin of the investor portal (shows other investors' data).

## Pre-meeting checklist (day before)

- [ ] Re-read their profile in INVESTOR-TARGET-LIST.md (portfolio, thesis, personalization angle)
- [ ] Prepare 2 questions specific to them (see OBJECTION-HANDLING "ask back" list)
- [ ] Update portal Traction metrics if new numbers exist
- [ ] Confirm meeting link + timezone; join 2 min early
- [ ] Have OUTREACH-TRACKER.csv open to log outcome immediately after

## The numbers you must know cold

- Raise: $500–750k target, $250k minimum viable, post-money SAFE
- Use of funds: 45% commercial / 35% engineering hire / 20% legal+infra
- Milestones: 100+ clinics, 500+ assessments, 3–5 paying clinics, 18-month runway
- Current honest metrics: {refresh weekly from tracker}
- Burn: ~$33k/month average post-raise
- Comps narrative: Hims/Ro (demand side proof), Zocdoc (directory economics), Clipboard/Vivian (healthcare staffing marketplaces), Toast/ServiceTitan (vertical OS pattern)

## Hard questions to expect (full answers in INVESTOR-OBJECTION-HANDLING.md)

Traction · solo founder · compliance · why-now-before-revenue · competition with Hims · how the wedge monetizes · what happens if Google/Hims builds this

## After the meeting (same day)

1. Log outcome + next action in OUTREACH-TRACKER.csv
2. Send thank-you within 24h containing: one thing you said you'd follow up on (do it), portal link reminder, and the specific next step you're proposing
3. If pass: ask one question — "what would make this fundable for you?" — log the answer
4. If interest: propose concrete next step (reference call with a listed clinic, deeper product session, or SAFE docs)

## Reference-call preparation (investors will ask eventually)

Line up 2–3 clinic contacts from the traction sprint willing to take a 10-minute investor reference call. Brief them honestly: "tell them exactly what you think — including what's missing."
