# 30-Day Traction Plan — Run in Parallel with Outreach

**Goal:** every investor follow-up (day 3 / 7 / 14) contains a *new true number*. This plan generates those numbers. All metrics feed the portal's Traction page via founder admin (`/investor/admin/metrics`) with honest "Actual" labels.

## What early healthcare investors actually weight (in order)

1. **Supply-side adoption velocity** — clinics saying yes, week over week. At pre-seed this beats revenue.
2. **Decision-maker conversations** — proof the founder can reach clinic owners repeatedly (the whole GTM depends on it).
3. **Activation depth** — clinics that complete profiles, add booking links, respond to leads (not just "agreed to listing").
4. **Demand signal** — assessments completed and routed; even hundreds matter if conversion is measured.
5. **Willingness-to-pay evidence** — pilots, LOIs, or explicit "I'd pay for X" quotes from calls.
6. **Founder learning rate** — call notes that changed the product/pitch. Investors fund loops, not snapshots.

Vanity metrics to avoid leading with: raw site traffic, social followers, unqualified waitlists.

## Weekly structure

### Week 1 — Pipeline and baseline
- Load/clean 150+ clinic prospects into Command Center (admin.novalyte.io) across 3–5 launch metros
- 40+ outreach calls/emails to clinics; log every outcome in the CRM
- Baseline metrics recorded: clinics in pipeline, profiles currently published, directory visitors (privacy-safe analytics), assessments completed to date
- Publish 2 journal articles targeting launch-metro search intent

**Targets:** 150 prospects loaded · 40 contacted · 5 decision-maker conversations · 3 listing agreements

### Week 2 — Conversion and activation
- 60+ additional clinic touches; second-touch follow-ups on week-1 conversations
- Convert agreements into published, verified profiles (photos, services, booking links)
- Launch 2–3 Campaign Studio landing pages for launch metros with embedded assessments
- Ask every engaged clinic one question: "Would you pay for qualified patient inquiries — and how much?" Log verbatim answers.

**Targets:** cumulative 100 contacted · 12 conversations · 8 agreements · 5 profiles live · 2 booking links added

### Week 3 — Demand and depth
- Drive first paid/organic traffic tests to landing pages (small budget, measure assessment start/completion)
- Route completed assessments to listed clinics; track clinic response
- Pitch 2–3 most-engaged clinics on a paid pilot (patient-acquisition or onboarding package); ask for an LOI if they won't start immediately
- Marketplace: invite 5–10 vendors (labs, supplements, billing) to apply for listings

**Targets:** cumulative 15 profiles live · 100+ assessments started · 40+ completed · 1 pilot conversation in writing · 3 vendor applications

### Week 4 — Proof assembly
- Consolidate: full-funnel numbers (visitor → assessment start → completion → routed lead → clinic response)
- Secure 1–2 signed LOIs or first paid pilot (even $500/mo counts — it converts the pitch from "will anyone pay?" to "how many will pay?")
- Write the "first 30 days of GTM" update — this becomes follow-up-2 content for every investor thread
- Update portal Traction page with all "Actual" metrics; upload GTM summary PDF to data room

**Targets:** 20+ profiles live · 200+ assessments completed · 1 paid pilot or 2 LOIs · investor update shipped

## Rules

- Never report a projection as an actual. The portal's status labels exist for this.
- Verbatim clinic quotes (anonymized) are gold in investor meetings — collect them deliberately.
- If a week misses targets, report the miss and the fix in the investor update anyway; the learning loop is the asset.
